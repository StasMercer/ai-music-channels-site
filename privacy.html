Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 15, 2026

AI Music Channels ("the app") is a personal tool operated by its owner to upload videos to the owner's own YouTube channels using the YouTube Data API.

Data accessed

When the owner authorizes the app with their Google account, it receives an OAuth token that allows it to upload videos, set thumbnails, and manage metadata on the owner's own YouTube channels.

How data is used

The token is used solely to perform those uploads and edits. The app does not collect, sell, share, or disclose any personal data of other users, and does not display ads.

Storage

OAuth tokens are stored only in the owner's private environment (local machine or private secrets store) and are never published or sent to third parties other than Google's APIs.

Google API Services

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
