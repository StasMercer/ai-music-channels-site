AI Music Channels

AI Music Channels is a small personal tool that creates long-form ambient music videos (rainy piano, late-night café jazz, dark academia classical) and uploads them to the owner's own YouTube channels. All music and visuals are AI-generated and disclosed as such on YouTube.

The tool is used only by its owner, to upload videos to channels the owner controls. It is not offered to the public.
